# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Octo Teacher a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy, respecting differing opinions and experiences, accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or imagery, personal attacks, trolling, insulting comments, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Child and Family Privacy

Never post real child names, faces, voices, device identifiers, family exports, private conversations, school records, or precise household activity in public project spaces. Maintainers may immediately remove material that creates a privacy or safety risk.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's security advisory channel. Do not include sensitive reports in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.
